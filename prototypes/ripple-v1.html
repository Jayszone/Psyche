Psyche

Every event is a butterfly. Find where it lands.

Drop an event in. Pick something close to you. See the chain of hops between them, who holds the information at each step, and how much of the signal is left by the time it arrives.

Examples

Chains are plausible reasoning, not measured fact. The signal left is the product of each link's strength (1 to 5, divided by 5) along the best route, so it only goes down as you move away from the event. Live traces are written by Claude from general knowledge, with no web lookup.
